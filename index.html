About

Career Summary

Rakshit H. Parikh is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Parul Institute of Engineering and Technology (Diploma Studies), Parul University, Vadodara, where he has taught since February 2017. He is currently pursuing a Ph.D. at Parul University, building on an M.Tech in Thermal Engineering (CGPA 8.06) and a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering (CGPA 7.77).


His research centres on thermal power-plant efficiency and cooling-system design, including an industry-defined dissertation project on air-cooled condensers funded by the Royal Academy of Engineering, delivered through the Newton-Bhabha research collaboration between L&T Knowledge City, the University of Surrey, and Parul University. He has authored three peer-reviewed publications and combines this research focus with hands-on teaching in thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, and engineering drawing.

Research Projects & Grants

M.Tech Dissertation — Optimization of Turbine Back Pressure (TBP) & Initial Temperature Difference (ITD) for Air-Cooled Condensers

B.E. Project — Thermoacoustic Refrigerators

Explored refrigeration using acoustic waves to pump heat across a temperature gradient, with no moving parts or harmful refrigerants — driven by electromagnetic loudspeakers generating the acoustic input.
